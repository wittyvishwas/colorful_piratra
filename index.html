Good to know

Gift hampers in Kota, answered

Where can I buy customised gift hampers in Kota?

At Colourful Pitara, 2nd Floor, A3, Kalakunj ki Gali, New Colony, Gumanpura, Kota. We're open every day from 11:30 am to 9 pm, and you can also message us on WhatsApp at +91 84510 05498.

Can you make a hamper to fit my budget?

Yes. Tell us the occasion, who it's for and how much you'd like to spend, and we'll choose the goodies and finishing touches around that.

Which occasions do you make hampers for?

Birthdays, weddings and anniversaries, Diwali, Rakhi and other festivals, baby showers and new arrivals, thank-yous, and fully custom themes.

Do you take bulk or corporate gift orders?

Yes. We make matching hampers in quantity, with names or initials on the tags, for weddings, events and companies.

How do I place an order?

Visit the store, or build your hamper on this page and send it to us on WhatsApp. We'll reply with ideas and a quote.
